## Join IEEE Graduate Student Branch

We welcome **all graduate students at Ohio State** who are interested in:

- Engineering & technology  
- Research & innovation  
- Professional development  
- Leadership & service  

## Open Positions

We’re currently looking for students interested in helping with:

- **Website Manager(s)** — maintain and update the club website, post announcements, keep pages current  
- **Treasurer** — manage budget, reimbursements, and IEEE/OSU funding paperwork  
- **Event Organizer** — plan technical talks/workshops, coordinate logistics, bookings, and scheduling  
- **Outreach Organizer** — promote events, manage recruitment, coordinate collaborations with departments and student groups  

> Don’t see the exact role you want? Reach out — we’re happy to match you with projects based on your interests.

## How to Get Involved

- Attend our events and workshops  
- Volunteer on organizing committees  
- Serve in leadership roles  
- Collaborate with industry and faculty partners
